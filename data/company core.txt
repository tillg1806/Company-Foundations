# Built from Endless Sky map planet data for normal fresh pilots.
# Eligible headquarters: 366 accessible visitable spaceports.

mission "Company Foundations: Starter Supplier Backfill: Shuttle"
	name "Company Starter Supplier Backfill"
	invisible
	landing
	repeat
	to offer
		has "cf: active"
		has "cf: shuttle"
		not "cf: ship available: Shuttle"
	on offer
		set "cf: ship available: Shuttle"
		log "Company Foundations" "Procurement Backfill" `The company records its starter Shuttle procurement channel as an available supplier for future simulated purchases.`
